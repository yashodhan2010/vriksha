# Presentation Masters

Slide size: 16:9 widescreen. Background defaults to warm paper `#fdfbf6`. Use Fraunces for large statements and Inter for support copy. Keep one idea per slide.

No slide master includes performance figures, return charts, testimonials, urgency, or regulatory logos.

## Master 1: Statement Slide

- Full warm paper background.
- Large Fraunces statement, left aligned, max 2 lines.
- Small monospace label above: `VRKSHA RESEARCH`.
- Optional 3px forest accent bar at left.
- Footer line with registration text in 9-10pt.

Use for: thesis, section message, decision framing.

## Master 2: One-Number Slide

- Large Fraunces number centered-left.
- Label below in Inter.
- Context card on right with 2-3 bullets.
- Permitted examples: `35 holdings`, `15 minutes a fortnight`, `14-day review cadence`.
- Prohibited examples: return, profit, outperformance, win rate.

## Master 3: Simple Diagram

- Three to five nodes connected by thin forest lines.
- White node cards, 12px radius.
- One highlighted node with pale green wash.
- Use for: universe -> filters -> ranking -> portfolio -> rebalance.

## Master 4: Comparison Table

- Forest header row, white text.
- Zebra rows.
- Two to four columns.
- Use for factual comparison: Vriksha does / Vriksha does not; self-directed / managed service.

## Master 5: Section Divider

- Full forest background.
- Large white Fraunces section title.
- Small gold line above title.
- No imagery.

## Master 6: Report Excerpt

- Warm paper background.
- Left column: document title and metadata.
- Right column: excerpt card with 3px forest accent.
- Use for reading a paragraph aloud without filling the whole slide.

## Master 7: Risk / Constraint Slide

- White card on warm paper.
- 3px orange accent bar for warning or red accent for stop.
- Plain title, then 3 bullet points.
- Use for: not-designed-for, implementation limits, regulatory caveats.

## Master 8: Process Timeline

- Horizontal stem line with 4-5 steps.
- Step labels in monospace uppercase.
- White content cards below each step.
- Use for onboarding or fortnightly update cadence.

## Master 9: Document Cover

- Warm paper.
- Small Vriksha wordmark/text top-left.
- Large Fraunces report title.
- Metadata block: category, date, author, registration number.
- 3px forest bar and small leaf-mark SVG.
- Legal footer in 9-10pt.

## Master 10: Closing Legal Slide

- Warm paper background.
- Title: "Disclosures"
- Full legal footer block set with readable line-height.
- Market-risk sentence at 10pt minimum.
- Verification link as text only.

## Export Notes

If rebuilt in PowerPoint or Figma:

- Use editable text and shapes.
- Keep tables editable.
- Use the `assets/leaf-mark.svg` only as a quiet motif.
- Avoid background images.
- Do not flatten legal copy into screenshots.
